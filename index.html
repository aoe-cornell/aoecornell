Our History

Founded November 13, 1983

Women engineering students at Marquette University came together to bring more women into engineering, and Alpha Omega Epsilon was born.

At Cornell, our founding Alpha class longed for a close-knit community for women in technical majors. In September 2017, twelve founding members were initiated, and the Beta Chi Chapter began.
